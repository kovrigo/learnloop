# The Odyssey: A Ten-Year Trip Home — internal shot plan

Frames are 1-based and inclusive. `python3 scripts/render_storyboard.py` fills the tokens from `script/timeline.json` into `分镜表.md`. Narration and subtitle blocks: `script/timeline.md`.

Total frames 12649 (30 fps). Chapter starts: C1 86 / C2 3167 / C3 5768 / C4 7565 / C5 9849. Ids S100 and up never resolve in `render_storyboard.py` (it matches only two-digit ids, `S\d\d`), so G8 writes those frames as numbers taken from `script/timeline.json`.

Style source: `brand/brandbook.md` and the approved frames `brand/examples/V1.png`, `V2.png`, `V3.png`. Approved frame code: `reference/mockups/make_scenes.py` and `make_revised.py` (clip paths, paper bodies, shadow and grain filters).

## Shot rules

- Shot ids are SC01–SC35 in video order, as the toolkit checks expect. One paragraph = one shot, unless a row says otherwise. Shot range = [first sentence from−8, next shot's first sentence from−9]. Last shot of a chapter, and of the video, ends at its last sentence to+2.
- The first shot starts at frame 1. No title card before the cold open. The LearnLoop sting comes after the cold open.
- Element entrances align to subtitle-block starts (the Beats column): no earlier than −6, no later than +3.
- Each shot's last 8 frames: every element leaves to α=0, or a paper wipe covers it. No hard cut on a visible element.
- Last beat landed → exit start ≥30 frames, target 30–45. Shots ending on a short last block (⚠ list below) pre-hang their last element on the previous block.
- No element stays fully still for more than 3 s. A talking head bobs on its speech beats; the boat rocks; dotted lines run. Never add idle float or breathing to a still object.
- One main subject per shot, at least 170 px tall, or a word at least 96 px. The main subject has the white paper border and the hard shadow. Supporting items are smaller, with the same border.
- Camera: at least 3 moves per chapter (push-in, follow pan, parallax slide, paper wipe). 30–45 frames, easeInOut. No camera move in the last beat: move end → exit start ≥30 frames.
- Safe areas: content y 90–620, x 40–1240. HUD row y 20–90. Subtitle band y 637–690 and progress bar y 687–720: background only, no text, no figure, no entrance path through them.

## Style replacing the toolkit defaults

- No black stage, no dot field, no star field, no fog: every shot paints a full-frame flat background (palette below) plus the paper grain overlay at 35–38% opacity. Turn the template backdrop off for the whole video.
- No purple, no glitch, no light sweep, no stage lines, no ghost text. Glitch whitelist: none. Light-sweep whitelist: none.
- "Light" on the main subject = white paper border (10–29 px at 1280 width, as in V1–V3) + hard shadow: `feDropShadow dx 8 dy 13 stdDeviation 8`, colour #16151F, opacity .27.
- Entrance "slap": from scale 1.15 and rotation ±4° to rest in 6 frames, easeOut, then a 4-frame settle wobble. Exit: peel (rotate 6°, slide 30 px, fade) or a paper wipe.
- Paper character = photo head (clip path + white border) on a drawn paper body (`paperHero`, `paperPenelope`, `paperCyclops` symbols in `make_scenes.py`). Head pivots at the neck: ±3–6° on speech beats. Arms are separate pieces pivoting at the shoulder.
- Speech bubble: cream #FAF7EE, white 10 px border, tail toward the speaker, opens from the tail in 8 frames.
- Paper tag: rotated −8…+8°, cream #F7E9D7, white 8 px border, Inter Black caps.
- Dotted connector: yellow #FFE38B, width 7–9, dash 3/19, round caps, dash offset animates.
- Museum object photo: shown whole, cut out or on a white photo border, slapped in like a print on a table. Never cropped to a face for a character.
- Type: Inter (OFL), Black for big words with letter-spacing −4 at 70 px+, ExtraBold/Bold for labels. HUD lines: Inter Black 18–19 px, caps, letter-spacing 2, white.

## Cast and assets

Heads are artistic stand-ins, never portraits of the named people. One head plays one role in the whole video. Every file goes in `public/assets/odyssey/img/` and gets a line in `public/assets/odyssey/img/MANIFEST.md`: file, role, museum, object title, object page, file URL, licence as stated on the museum page, date checked.

Heads approved by the owner (The Met, public domain, Open Access):

- Odysseus: 255427 "Marble head of a bearded man from a stele (grave marker)". https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/255427
- Polyphemus: 252511 "Marble head of a god, probably Zeus", with the drawn one-eye band from V1. https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/252511
- Penelope: 249052 "Marble head of a girl from a small statue". https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/249052

Heads still to choose (Met Open Access `isPublicDomain: true`, or another museum on the research list): Athena (candidate 258077 "Athena Medici"), Calypso, Circe's victims (one man head, reused in Circe's before/after only), Telemachus (a youth), Mentor (an old man), the maid, two Phaeacian listeners (SC05), the suitors (a crowd role: 3–4 heads shared by all suitors, e.g. 251524 and 254640).
